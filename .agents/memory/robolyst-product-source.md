Robolyst's official product is at `https://robolyst.org/`; its team toolkit is described as available to FTC, FRC, FLL, and VEX teams. FTC competition sync, scouting, metrics, and public team pages are the FTC-specific features. `www.robolyst.com` is only a minimal splash page with the line “AI Driven. Risk Defined.” The Robotics Catalyst Foundation site also links to the `.org` platform as its product.

**Why:** A comparison limited to the `.com` homepage incorrectly concluded that Robolyst had no public feature or pricing information; describing the full toolkit as FTC-only also omits its published FRC, FLL, and VEX audience.

**How to apply:** For comparisons involving Robolyst, use its `.org` product and toolkit pages to check audience, its FTC scouting and data pages for competition-specific features, and Help for current fees; link to the sources and date the review.
