Robolyst's FTC team-management, scouting, and competition-data product is at `https://robolyst.org/`. `www.robolyst.com` is only a minimal splash page with the line “AI Driven. Risk Defined.” The Robotics Catalyst Foundation site also links to the `.org` platform as its product.

**Why:** A comparison limited to the `.com` homepage incorrectly concluded that Robolyst had no public feature or pricing information.

**How to apply:** For comparisons involving Robolyst, use its `.org` product, scouting, competition-data, and help pages; link to those sources and date the review.
