---
name: Parts source coverage
description: Evidence and completeness rules for the robotics parts supplier catalog.
---

Treat keyless search results as discovery, not as an exhaustive vendor catalog. Verified unauthenticated official product feeds were found for Andymark, Ferra, Optii, SWYFT Robotics, and Melonbotics. Sync may also probe a supplier-owned standard Shopify `/products.json` endpoint for other vendors, but probing alone is not evidence of coverage: require a valid Shopify product payload and verified pagination to the end before marking a catalog synced. Public sitemaps are candidate URL lists, not proof of a complete sellable catalog; observed sitemaps can include general pages and service content. McMaster-Carr has a large public sitemap index, but URL discovery does not establish public SKU pricing or remove its approved-customer API requirement. The goBILDA API found during research was an independent third-party wrapper, not an official feed. Some machining suppliers sell configurable fabrication services rather than standard SKU inventories.

McMaster-Carr's published terms prohibit automated scraping or indexing to build a similar, competitive, or replacement product database; its API terms also limit use to internal company systems and require written consent for sharing with third parties. Do not ingest McMaster sitemap or page data into this catalog without written permission. MISUMI's catalog pages were blocked with HTTP 403 from this environment, and no unauthenticated bulk API was confirmed. BigCommerce product sitemaps are available for goBILDA, ServoCity, and REV Robotics via `xmlsitemap.php?type=products&page=1`, but they provide URL inventories rather than item records; their robots files also name AI crawlers as disallowed, and ServoCity/REV specify a 10-second crawl delay. Do not treat those sitemaps as public full-feed permission or bypass the declared crawl policy.

**Why:** The user requires catalog sourcing from public sources without relying on a user-provided CSV, and explicitly wants coverage claims tied to evidence. Search snippets, unofficial APIs, or stale third-party datasets can omit or misidentify products.

**How to apply:** During explicit sync, a standard Shopify endpoint can be tested on the supplier's own domain. Add it to full-feed coverage only after validating the payload, preserving each sellable variant's SKU or stable vendor identifier, and reaching a reliable pagination end. Review each supplier's terms and robots policy before turning sitemap URLs into a crawler. Otherwise keep that supplier searchable, label its catalog coverage as unavailable or partial, and state the limitation plainly.
