The product serves multiple robotics teams; Team 23442 is the builder, not the only intended customer. Public descriptions should say it is made by Team 23442 for robotics teams like the reader's.

**Why:** The user clarified that the product is not limited to one team after the meta description “A shared workspace for Team 23442” implied otherwise.

**How to apply:** In public SEO, landing, about, pricing, and social-preview copy, frame Team 23442 as creator attribution and state the broader robotics-team audience. Preserve accurate team-specific names inside a team's workspace and in origin-story assets.
